About Enterprise

Centenary Heritage & State Quality

OJSC 'Food Plant Veselovo' is a premier agro-industrial manufacturer in Belarus specializing in natural fruit wines, ciders, low-alcohol beverages, and refined potato starches.

🏛️ Corporate Status

Member of 'MINSK KRISTALL GROUP' holding under Decree No. 326 of the President of the Republic of Belarus. Subordinate to state concern 'BELGOSPISCHEPROM'.

📜 Quality Certifications

Certified under ISO 9001-2015 quality management system and HACCP food safety standards, guaranteeing rigorous control from farm raw materials to finished bottle.

🍎 Raw Materials & Traditions

Built upon 100% natural Belarusian fruits and berries (apples, chokeberry, blackcurrant, blueberry, cranberry) harvested from ecologically clean regions.

Historical Milestones

From 1925 Agricultural Processing to Modern Holding

1925 – 1927

Plant Foundation

Construction commenced in Veselovo village. In 1927, agricultural processing workshop launched with 20 tons/day potato processing capacity.

1930s – 1960s

Wine Workshop Expansion

Inauguration of wine production facilities and fruit juice processing lines, setting regional standards for fortified fruit wines.

2000s – 2015

Holding Integration

Modernization of production technology. In July 2015, joined 'MINSK KRISTALL GROUP' holding under Presidential Decree No. 326.

2020s – Present

New Portfolio & Ciders

Launch of 'Apple Boom' ciders, 'Chinazes' natural RTD beverages, lifestyle youth drinks, and premium GOST starches.

Logistics & Storage

Storage Conditions & Shelf Stability

🍷 Wine & Vermouth Storage

Store in ventilated, dark premises free from foreign odors at temperatures between +5°C and +20°C. Relative humidity not exceeding 85%.

🍏 Cider & RTD Storage

Store in dark dry facilities at temperatures between +2°C and +20°C. Protect from direct sunlight. Shelf life: 12 to 16 months.

🥔 Starch Packaging & Storage

Store in dry, clean warehouses at max 75% relative humidity. Transported in 40kg PP bags with inner PE liners or 500g retail packets. Shelf life: 24 months.
